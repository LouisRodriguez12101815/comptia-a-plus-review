Assumes ordinary distance-vector updates; filtering/interface eligibility can further restrict advertisements.
- **R4 — switching:** Cisco Nexus 7000 NX-OS Layer 2 Switching, Layer 2 Ethernet Switching Overview / Switching Frames Between Segments / Building the Address Table. https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/switches/datacenter/sw/nx-os/layer2/configuration/guide/b_Cisco_Nexus_7000_Series_NX-OS_Layer_2_Switching_Configuration_Guide/m_configuring_layer_2_ethernet_switching.html . Explicitly describes LAN-port collision-domain separation, source learning, destination lookup and same-VLAN flooding excluding ingress. - **R5 — VLANs:** Cisco IOS XE Configure VLAN, opening definition / Benefits of VLANs / Logical Networks. https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/switches/lan/c9000/lyr2-fwd/vlan/vlan-configuration-guide/configure-vlan.html . Separate broadcast domains; inter-VLAN routing via SVIs. Not a universal security-policy guarantee.
- **R6 — voice VLAN modes:** Cisco Configure Catalyst 2960/2950 with Voice VLAN, Voice VLAN Overview / Configure. https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/switches/catalyst-2950-series-switches/113260-voice-vlan-00.html . Historical Catalyst/phone examples; do not assume every modern platform supports every command. Current C9350 documentation explicitly restricts dot1p/VLAN-0 support: https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/switches/lan/c9000/lyr2-fwd/vlan/vlan-configuration-guide/configure-voice-vlan.html , Restrictions for Voice VLANs / Cisco IP Phone Voice Traffic.
- **R7 — ordinary classic-STP startup / PortFast:** Cisco Understand Spanning Tree PortFast and BPDU Guard Features, Background Information / PortFast and BPDU Guard. https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/lan-switching/spanning-tree-protocol/10586-65.pdf . About 30 seconds with traditional 802.1D; PortFast bypasses listening/learning for suitable end-station ports.
- **R8 — a 50-second recovery case:** Cisco Troubleshoot STP Problems, Packet Corruption. https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/lan-switching/spanning-tree-protocol/10556-16.html . From last superior BPDU: max age 20 + listening 15 + learning 15. Not every topology change follows this case.
- **R9 — default classic timers:** Cisco Understand and Tune STP Timers, Spanning Tree Protocol Timers / Default Values. https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/lan-switching/spanning-tree-protocol/19120-122.html . Classic 802.1D scope, not a universal RSTP timing rule.
- **R10 — CoS versus queues:** Cisco Catalyst 2960 IOS 15.0(2)SE QoS, Understanding QoS (3-bit User Priority, values 0–7): https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/switches/lan/catalyst2960/software/release/15-0_2_se/configuration/guide/scg2960/swqos.html . Counterexample to universal eight queues: Cisco 2960-CX/3560-CX IOS 15.2(3)E QoS, Restrictions for QoS (four default egress queues, optional eight): https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/switches/lan/catalyst2960cx_3650cx/software/release/15-2_3_e/configuration/guide/b_1523e_consolidated_2960cx_3560cx_cg/b_consolidated_152ex_2960-X_cg_chapter_011100.html . Both accessed via web reader; a direct local HTTP request to the former returned 403, not treated as a successful local download.
