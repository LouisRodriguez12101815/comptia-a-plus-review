All 36 steps on one map

Each lane is a device and each numbered marker is one of the 36 steps, in order from top to bottom. The green arrows are the four moments a frame travels on a cable. Notice how many steps are decisions and lookups, and how the IP addresses stay the same while the MAC addresses change on every arrow.
