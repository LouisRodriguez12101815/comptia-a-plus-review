Review question 20 (answer A) and exam objective 2.1. An autonomous system is a collection of networks or subnets in the same administrative domain. An IGP runs inside an autonomous system. An EGP, such as BGP, runs between them. A router prefers the longest prefix first, then the lowest administrative distance, then the lowest metric.
