Cloud benefits and economics decisions

Read the requirement cue before naming the benefit. These are enabled choices, not guaranteed outcomes.

Cloud benefit decision tree

Demand changes unexpectedly?
→ Elasticity: dynamically adjust capacity.
Workload growth is planned?
→ Scalability: handle a larger workload.
Provision or experiment quickly?
→ Agility: reduce procurement delay.
Users or data span geographies?
→ Global reach: choose suitable Regions or supported edge placement.
Continue through failure?
→ Availability, resilience or fault tolerance; architecture still matters.
Provider operates at huge scale?
→ Economies of scale may help; savings are not guaranteed.

Cloud economics decision tree

Resource is oversized?
→ Rightsize from measured requirements.
Resource is idle?
→ Stop, remove or schedule it when retention and availability allow.
Peak capacity was bought in advance?
→ Evaluate utilization and variable consumption.
Purchase price hides facilities or staffing?
→ Compare Total Cost of Ownership.
Maintenance burden is high?
→ Compare managed-service price with labor and reliability tradeoffs.
License has host or portability limits?
→ Check licensing and tenancy before choosing a service.

Keep the pairs separate

PairExam-safe distinction
Elasticity / scalabilityDynamic demand response / capacity for workload size.
Agility / elasticityFast provisioning and experimentation / capacity adjustment.
Availability / durabilityAccess when needed / persistence of data.
Rightsizing / autoscalingChoose suitable size / change quantity or capacity over time.
TCO / monthly billAll ownership and operating categories / one billing view.
Cheapest price / optimizationDirect unit price / measured tradeoff among cost, operations and requirements.
