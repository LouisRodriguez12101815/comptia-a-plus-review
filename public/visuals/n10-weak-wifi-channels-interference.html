2.4 GHz channels and interference

Pick two access point channels and see whether they overlap. Same channel means co-channel interference; partly overlapping means adjacent-channel interference.



Rules to keep. In 2.4 GHz only channels 1, 6 and 11 avoid each other. Same channel is co-channel interference: devices take turns. Partly overlapping channels are adjacent-channel interference, which is worse because frames are corrupted.
