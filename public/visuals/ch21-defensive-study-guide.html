Attack evidence: identify the layer that changed

Network+ N10-009 · Chapter 21. These are evidence distinctions, not proof from a single symptom. Read the lesson, then practice with the fictional written exercise.

Observation → mechanism → impact

Lease options

An unauthorized DHCP service supplies a different router or resolver. Clients may lose service or take an attacker-controlled path.

IP-to-MAC binding

Forged ARP messages associate a legitimate IP with an attacker-controlled MAC. That supports ARP poisoning, not merely copying an interface address.

Name-resolution data

A forged DNS answer may redirect a connection. Establish where false data persists before identifying a particular poisoned cache.

Wireless identity

An evil twin impersonates a network. A duplicate SSID by itself is insufficient: legitimate APs can share it too.
