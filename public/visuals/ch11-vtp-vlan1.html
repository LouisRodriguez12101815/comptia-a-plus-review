VTP and protected VLANs

VLAN Trunking Protocol (VTP) copies the VLAN database from a server switch to its client switches. It does not negotiate trunks. Try adding a VLAN on the server, then try deleting VLAN 1.

The VTP server holds the master VLAN database.

Review questions 14, 18, and 20. VTP propagates the VLAN database, not the trunking database. Every port starts in VLAN 1 from the factory. VLANs 1 and 1002 through 1005 are protected by the IOS and cannot be deleted.
