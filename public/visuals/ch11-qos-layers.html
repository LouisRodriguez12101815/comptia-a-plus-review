Which QoS marking works at which layer?

Quality of service marks traffic so it gets priority. Pick a method to see the layer it works at and the field that holds the mark. Then pick a CoS value to see its queue.

Review question 3 (answer D) and written lab 10. CoS is the Layer 2 QoS marking. It is a 3-bit field inside the 802.1Q tag, which gives eight queues. ToS and DiffServ are Layer 3 methods in the IP header.
