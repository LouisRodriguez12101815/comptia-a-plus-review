Permissions

Policies define allowed or denied actions. Least privilege narrows actions and resources to the task.

Extra login factor

MFA strengthens authentication. It does not define authorization.

Group versus role

A group organizes IAM users. A role is assumable and commonly supplies temporary credentials.
