Classic STP recovery: a 50-second scenario

This example starts when a blocked port loses its last superior BPDU: 20 seconds of max-age expiry, then 15 seconds listening and 15 seconds learning. A newly active designated port without PortFast normally needs only the two forward-delay periods, about 30 seconds. Default classic 802.1D timers are assumed.

Blocking
20 s
Listening
15 s
Learning
15 s

Do not memorize 50 seconds for every port. Here the recovery waits for max age: 20 + 15 + 15 = 50 seconds. A new designated port with default timers and no PortFast normally spends 15 seconds listening and 15 seconds learning. Sources: Cisco PortFast background and STP troubleshooting, Packet Corruption.
