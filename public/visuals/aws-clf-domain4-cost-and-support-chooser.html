Domain 4: choose by the requirement

Pricing and capacity

  • No commitment → On-Demand
  • Interruptible spare capacity → Spot
  • Steady eligible usage/commitment → Savings Plans / RI pricing
  • Guarantee launch capacity → Capacity Reservation
  • Host-level visibility/licensing → Dedicated Host
  • Dedicated tenancy without host control → Dedicated Instance

Cost question

  • Estimate planned cost → Pricing Calculator
  • Analyze current/historical spend → Cost Explorer
  • Alert on a threshold → Budgets
  • Detailed reporting dataset → Cost and Usage Report
  • Team/project attribution → Activated cost allocation tags
  • Multiple-account billing → Organizations

Support or signal

  • Reference → Documentation/whitepapers
  • Troubleshooting → Knowledge Center / re:Post
  • Patterns → Prescriptive Guidance
  • Case → Support Center
  • Recommendation → Trusted Advisor
  • Health event → AWS Health
  • Abuse report → Trust and Safety

Current support tier

  • General account/billing/documentation baseline → Basic Support
  • Production 24/7 technical support → Business Support+
  • Strategic business-critical guidance/TAM → Enterprise Support
  • Highest proactive mission-critical operations help → Unified Operations
Do not conflateDistinction
Discount vs capacityRI/Savings Plans can discount eligible usage; Capacity Reservations address launch capacity.
Calculator vs Explorer vs BudgetsEstimate planned cost; analyze actual spend; alert on thresholds.
Trusted Advisor vs HealthRecommendations/checks versus account or service health events.
Marketplace vs Professional ServicesThird-party products/procurement versus AWS-delivered project expertise.
Current vs legacy support plansBasic, Business Support+, Enterprise Support, and Unified Operations are the current study cues; Developer Support, Business Support, and Enterprise On-Ramp are transitional legacy names.
