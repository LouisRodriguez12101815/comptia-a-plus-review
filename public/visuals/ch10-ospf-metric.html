OSPF metric: cost from bandwidth

OSPF's metric is cost. Cisco calculates it from the reference bandwidth (100 Mbps) divided by the interface bandwidth. Faster links have lower cost, so OSPF prefers them.

Cost = 100 Mbps ÷ interface bandwidth
