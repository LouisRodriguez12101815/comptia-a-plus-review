SDN planes, VXLAN and SD-WAN local breakout

Four ideas from objective 1.8 that the practice quiz keeps mixing up. Pick a view and read the picture, then the rule underneath it.

Rules to keep. The control plane builds the routing and switching tables, the data plane forwards using them, and the management plane is how you configure the device. VXLAN is UDP port 4789 with a 24-bit VNI. Local breakout sends trusted application traffic straight to the cloud instead of through headquarters.
