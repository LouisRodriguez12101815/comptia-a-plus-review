Wi-Fi 6, 6E and 7, and what changes when you roam

Pick a generation to see its standard, bands and headline feature. Then try roaming: the BSSID changes, the ESSID does not.

Rules to keep. 802.11ax is Wi-Fi 6. Wi-Fi 6E is the same standard used in the 6 GHz band. 802.11be is Wi-Fi 7, which adds multi-link operation. BSS coloring is a Wi-Fi 6 feature. Roaming changes the BSSID inside one ESSID.
