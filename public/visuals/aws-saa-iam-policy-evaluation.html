IAM policy evaluation: identity vs resource policies

One question classifies every policy: what is it attached to? Watch the 63-second explainer, then read the comparison map.

Narrated explainer: identity-based policies travel with the caller; resource-based policies sit on the target and name their principals.

Definition, classification, use case, cost

AspectIdentity-basedResource-based
DefinitionA policy attached to an IAM user, group, or role defining what that identity can doA policy attached to the resource itself defining who may access it and what they may do
ClassificationManaged or inlineInline only
Use caseGovern what your own identities can do across all resourcesGrant cross-account access (or anonymous access) without switching roles
Cost implicationNo AWS fee; managed policy size limit 6,144 charactersNo AWS fee; one policy document per resource, 20 KB limit for most services

Sources: AWS IAM User Guide — Identity-based policies and resource-based policies; JSON policy document size limits. Both policy types are free; the cost is management overhead as attachments grow.
