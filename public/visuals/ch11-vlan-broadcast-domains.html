VLANs split broadcast domains

Each VLAN is its own broadcast domain. Change a port's VLAN, pick a sender, and see which hosts receive its broadcast.

Review questions 7, 8, and 10. Adding VLANs increases the number of broadcast domains, and a broadcast never leaves its VLAN because it cannot cross a router. The book's answer to question 10 is B: more broadcast domains and more collision domains.
