Mobile-device components and service boundaries

This schematic shows common assemblies and diagnostic boundaries. Component placement is illustrative; exact part layout and service procedures vary by model.

Text equivalent

  • The display may combine panel, touch digitizer, camera, microphone, antennas, and cables; service boundaries differ by model.
  • The battery, memory, storage, wireless card, and keyboard may be separately replaceable or integrated. Confirm the exact parts list and service guide.
  • Record evidence, verify compatibility, follow manufacturer safety steps, and run the model’s post-service checks. Stop and escalate suspected battery damage.
