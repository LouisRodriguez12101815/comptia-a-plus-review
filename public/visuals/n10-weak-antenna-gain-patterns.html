Antenna gain reshapes the signal

Slide the gain and watch the coverage pattern change shape. Gain never adds power: the same energy goes somewhere else.


Rules to keep. Gain in dBi reshapes existing energy and adds no power. Higher gain on an omnidirectional antenna flattens the donut: more reach sideways, less above and below. Ceiling access points use omnidirectional antennas; long building-to-building links use highly directional ones.
