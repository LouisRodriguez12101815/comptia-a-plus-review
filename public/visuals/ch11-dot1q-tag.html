Inside an 802.1Q tagged frame

802.1Q adds a 4-byte (32-bit) tag between the source MAC address and the type field. Select any part of the frame to read what it does. Widths are not to scale.

Inside the 802.1Q tag (32 bits)

Review questions 3, 4, and 16. 802.1Q is the open-standard trunking protocol, so it works between vendors. The tag holds a 16-bit TPID of 0x8100, a 3-bit CoS field, a 1-bit drop-eligible indicator, and a 12-bit VLAN ID. The 3-bit CoS field is the layer 2 QoS marking.
