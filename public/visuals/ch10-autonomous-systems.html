Interior gateway protocols and autonomous systems

Routers inside one autonomous system (AS) share routes and are managed as a unit. Different ASs connect through exterior routing.

Inside an AS

Routers exchange routes to destination networks and converge as one unit, so they run the same interior protocol.

Between ASs

Each AS has its own number. Exterior routing, such as BGP, connects them.
