Which protocol is link-state?

Link-state routers track the state of every link and build a full map of the network. The other types learn routes from their neighbors instead.

Link-state

OSPF

Every router learns the state of every link, builds the same topology map, then runs Dijkstra to choose the shortest path.

Answer A
Hybrid

EIGRP

Distance-vector behavior with some link-state features. This is why it is often mistaken for link-state.

Distance-vector

RIP and IGRP

Learn routes from neighbors and pass their tables along. No full topology map.
